## Not hardware-tested

No keyboard was flashed. Comfort, real switch scanning, host repeat, Bluetooth/
USB transport, reconnection, sleep/wake, per-half bootloader entry, live VS Code/
Neovim text entry and Windows remote forwarding/Num Lock remain unverified.
The simulator masks SYS hardware commands and omits the Bluetooth-clear combo;
recovery locality was verified in source and compiled, not exercised on devices.

Use the [hardware checklist](setup.md#hardware-acceptance). Local layout-table
translation is stronger than assuming a US keycode means a Czech digit, but does
not establish every application's or remote client's behavior.
